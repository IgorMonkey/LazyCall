# Community conduct

[Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome contributors regardless of experience, background, or use of assistive tools. Discuss code and observed behaviour; do not insult people, harass participants, or publish private information.

Use issues and pull requests for work relevant to LazyCall. Explain disagreements with evidence and let maintainers resolve scope and review decisions.

Report problematic public content through GitHub's reporting tools. For a security or privacy problem, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and keep sensitive details private. Maintainers may moderate comments or restrict participation to keep the project usable and safe.
